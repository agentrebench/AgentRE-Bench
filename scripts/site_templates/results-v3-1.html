Scoring and scope

Scores: Average all five planned attempts for each artifact, then give all 20 artifacts equal weight. Refusals and budget exhaustion remain zero in Overall. Answer quality is shown separately with its actual coverage; refused answers are not assigned invented accuracy.

Encryption: Algorithms and keys are scored for each required component. Level 23 requires the custom file transform and XOR string decoding. A key’s credit is multiplied by its algorithm score; incomplete algorithm descriptions can therefore reduce both fields.

Result type: DeepSeek adds 100 new episodes collected November 4–5. All 600 saved answers then received the same versioned exact-claim review, with no inference reruns. The original 500 episodes had previously been rescored from v3.2/scorer 5.0 to v3.4/scorer 6.2.0; their numerical results remain unchanged here. Source report and review-ledger hashes are in the downloads. The retired single-attempt v3 leaderboard is not mixed into this release.

Review: A separate AI agent inspected all 219 exact-claim mappings and reproduced every score. The verdict is qualified: these are correct versioned rubric scores, not a pure factual-accuracy measure. Forty-six source-supported mappings still incur fixed-rubric penalties for observations outside allowed facts or placed in another field. No episode earned full credit (0/600); valid-answer coverage is reported separately.

Run outcomes: DeepSeek retained two zero-score budget failures and had no terminal infrastructure failures. Two transport errors (URLError) recovered through identical-request retries; no episode was discarded. Stopped Kimi and GLM campaigns are incomplete and excluded.

Evidence: Numeric records for all 600 episodes support the downloadable aggregates. Independent AI audit summary records the checks and limitations. The original October five-model download is preserved.

Scope: Twenty public artifacts represent ten programs in stripped and unstripped forms. Five attempts estimate repeat variability on this fixed suite. They do not establish generalization to unseen binary families, and public-task contamination cannot be ruled out.

Winner: Muse has the highest recorded Overall mean in this evaluation. Its 0.3243-point lead over Astra is not statistically established: the simultaneous 95% interval spans @@PAIR_LOW@@ to @@PAIR_HIGH@@ points and includes zero. The ranking describes these recorded results; it does not establish repeat-run superiority or practical equivalence. Conditional answer quality covers different answered subsets and is not a separate capability ranking.
